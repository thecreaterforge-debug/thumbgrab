Questions

Which size should I pick?

Use 1280x720 (HD) when it is available. It is the sharpest. Smaller sizes are good for quick previews or reference.

Why is the HD size missing?

Some videos, mostly older or low-resolution ones, have no 1280x720 thumbnail. The tool only shows sizes that exist.

Can I reuse a thumbnail?

A thumbnail belongs to the creator of the video. Use downloads for reference, inspiration or your own videos. Do not repost someone else's thumbnail as your own.
